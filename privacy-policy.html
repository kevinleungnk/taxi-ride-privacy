Privacy Policy

Applies to the app “Taxi & Ride Test / 的士網約車筆試通” (package hk.taxiridetest.app). Last updated: 22 October 2026.

  • No account is needed, and the developer does not collect your name, phone number, email or other personal details.
  • The app shows ads using Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent on first use and can change it later under Settings → 廣告私隱選項. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • The question bank is built in and works offline; only ads need a network connection.
  • Your answers, wrong-answer list, bookmarks and mock-exam results are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app or resetting progress in the app deletes them.
  • This is an independent study tool. It is not developed by, affiliated with, or endorsed by the Transport Department or the Government of the Hong Kong SAR.
